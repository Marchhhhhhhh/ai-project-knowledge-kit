# Draft Inbox

Use this directory to preserve authentic ideas, questions, and requests from before they are discussed. They do not need to be complete or mature.

- Recommended filename: `YYYYMMDD_sequence.md`, such as `20261117_01.md`.
- One file may contain multiple topics, each under its own heading.
- Images are allowed. A suggested location is `Drafts/Images/`, referenced with relative paths.
- A draft is not a confirmed requirement. Undiscussed and unconfirmed content remains pending.
- Do not delete an original draft after one of its topics is confirmed and archived. Processing status belongs in `../Progress/Progress.md`, where the index distinguishes processed and pending topics.
- Conclusions raised directly in conversation do not require a draft to be added here afterward.

You may keep this guide after integrating the kit into a project, or remove it once contributors are familiar with the workflow.
